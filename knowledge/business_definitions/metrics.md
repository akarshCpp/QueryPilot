# Business Definitions and Metrics

## Gross Revenue
Gross Revenue is the total amount of money generated by all completed sales, before any deductions for discounts or refunds.
Calculation: Sum of (`quantity` * `unit_price`) for all sales associated with 'Completed' orders.

## Net Revenue
Net Revenue is the actual money retained by the business after accounting for all deductions.
Calculation: Gross Revenue - Discounts - Refunds.
Note: Cancelled or Pending orders must not be included in Net Revenue calculations.

## Profit
Profit is the financial gain from sales after subtracting the cost of goods sold (COGS).
Calculation: Net Revenue - Sum of (`quantity` * `cost`) for all completed sales, excluding returned items.

## Average Order Value (AOV)
The average total amount spent per order.
Calculation: Net Revenue / Total number of 'Completed' orders.
